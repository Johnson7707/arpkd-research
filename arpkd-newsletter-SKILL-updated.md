## Citation Standard — Non-Negotiable

Every factual claim in every newsletter issue must have a full academic citation.

**Required format — ALL FOUR elements, every time:**
> Author(s) · *Journal Name* · Year · DOI: xx.xxxx/xxxxxx

**Correct:**
> Han CY et al. *Signal Transduction and Targeted Therapy* 2026. DOI: 10.1038/s41392-026-02796-0

**Incorrect — never acceptable:**
> "Signal Transduction and Targeted Therapy" — journal only
> "Masyuk 2017" — no journal or DOI
> "Established biology" — no source
> "Per UpToDate" — not a primary source; find the underlying paper

**In the newsletter specifically:**
- Full citation appears as the tag line ABOVE the finding title — not buried in body text
- Every number and percentage traces to a named paper
- UpToDate, Cleveland Clinic, company websites are NOT valid citations — find the primary source
- Conference/unpublished data must be labeled "conference data, not peer-reviewed"
- Personal communications must be labeled "personal communication, [Name], [Date]"

---

## Core Philosophy

**The ARPKD Research document already contains all the substance.** Every researcher has it. The newsletter is not a summary of what we know — it is a changelog of what's new since the last time they received the document.

Each issue answers one question: **"What changed in ARPKD Research since you last got it?"**

This means:
- Issue 1 covers July 25 → August 7 only (everything before July 24 is already in v1.5)
- Issue 2 covers August 8 → September 7 only
- And so on

If nothing significant changed in a month, the newsletter is very short. That is correct — a short newsletter is better than padding it with content already in the document.

**The document is the research. The newsletter is the update notification.**

## What Triggers a Newsletter Entry

Run through this checklist whenever a new finding is encountered during a research session. If YES to any of the following, add to the newsletter log:

**Automatic entries:**
- New peer-reviewed paper with a direct ARPKD mechanism, therapeutic, or clinical finding
- New clinical trial registration or status change for any ARPKD-relevant drug
- New researcher joining the network (name + institution + why relevant)
- Major correction to existing framework information
- New synthesis paper version (always include)
- FDA regulatory decision on an adjacent biliary disease drug (PBC, PSC, BA)

**Conditional entries (include if significant):**
- New drug showing benefit in adjacent PKD or biliary model (not yet ARPKD-tested)
- Conference abstract or preprint with compelling preliminary data
- Safety finding relevant to any drug in the therapeutic landscape
- New delivery platform or gene therapy advance relevant to PKHD1

**Do NOT include:**
- News articles, press releases, or opinion pieces without peer-reviewed backing
- Findings already in a prior newsletter issue
- Administrative updates (correspondence, grants) — those go in emails, not newsletters
- Anything the specific researcher already knows (their own published work)
