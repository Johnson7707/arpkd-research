This newsletter documents our systematic research effort into ARPKD with congenital hepatic fibrosis (CHF). We track the global research landscape, identify therapeutic candidates, map cross-connections between findings, and maintain a network of 45 investigators across 12 countries working on biliary disease, gene therapy, liver organoids, and PKD biology.

All issues are freely available below. To receive future issues by email, contact nathaniel_johnson@yahoo.com.

October 2026 · Issue 3 In Progress — Sends Oct 1
Gene Therapy Reaches Human Liver · Biliary Fibrosis Mechanism Genetically Proven · FDA Approves First Pediatric Ultra-Rare Liver Gene Therapy
The world's first in vivo liver base editing Phase 1 trial demonstrates 74% sustained gene correction at 24 weeks with no serious adverse events — direct proof-of-concept for PKHD1 base editing in humans. A landmark Nature Metabolism paper genetically proves p38γ/δ is the obligate cholangiocyte fibrosis mediator via knockout in 1,003 patients across 4 cohorts, powerfully confirming our framework's p38δ cascade node. FDA grants accelerated approval for the first pediatric ultra-rare liver gene therapy using a surrogate endpoint — the regulatory template ARPKD-CHF programs need.

SPTAN1/RAC1 Confirmed in Human ARPKD · SPP1 Fifth Independent Confirmation · Primary Biliary Basement Membrane Defect Identified
Morizane's lab confirms SPTAN1/RAC1 in human ARPKD kidney organoids. SPP1 receives its fifth independent confirmation as a biliary fibrosis mediator. Waddell and Boulter (Edinburgh) identify the primary ECM defect in ARPKD biliary disease — laminin and collagen IV loss from the basement membrane preceding fibrosis.
