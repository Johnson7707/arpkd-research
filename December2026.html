🧬 Molecular Biology & Biliary Cascade

🧬 MOLECULAR BIOLOGY & BILIARY CASCADE

⭐ FINDING — Collagen I is an active instructor of cholangiocyte specification and tubulogenesis: excess collagen I in ARPKD-CHF may drive abnormal ductal plate formation as a developmental feedback loop. ⭐ FINDING · Nov 2, 2026

Source: Thelwall IG, Morell CM, Dziedzicka D, Cabriales L, Hodgson A, Roos FJM, Elfari L, Vallier L, Chalut KJ. "Mechanical signalling through collagen I regulates cholangiocyte specification and tubulogenesis during liver development." Development 2026. Preprint: bioRxiv 2025.01.22.633528. Vallier lab (Charité Berlin) + Chalut lab (Cambridge). Ludovic Vallier is on this newsletter's distribution list.

What they did: Used StemBond hydrogels — collagen I-coated at varying stiffnesses — to investigate the influence of the ECM microenvironment on cholangiocyte differentiation from hiPSCs. Found that collagen I strongly promotes cholangiocyte specification (KRT19+) independently of matrix stiffness. Identified integrin α2β1 (ITGα2β1) as the primary mechanoreceptor co-localizing with cholangiocyte marker KRT19. Showed that inhibiting ITGα2β1-collagen I binding during tubulogenesis produces aberrant bile duct tube formation. First demonstration that collagen type I is essential for both biliary epithelial cell fate choice and morphogenesis.

⭐ ARPKD-CHF developmental feedback loop: In ARPKD-CHF, FPC loss → excess collagen I production via CTGF/TGF-β cascade → elevated periportal collagen I → abnormal bias toward cholangiocyte specification in liver progenitors → defective biliary tubulogenesis = ductal plate malformation. Collagen I excess in ARPKD-CHF may actively worsen ductal plate malformation by misguiding progenitor fate decisions — independently of FPC's direct effects on primary cilia signaling. This is a new mechanistic connection linking fibrotic ECM remodeling to developmental biliary pathology.

Connection to existing framework: Waddell/Boulter 2026 (Dis Model Mech): ECM primary defect in CHF is laminin/collagen IV LOSS; Yasoshima 2009 (J Pathol): basement membrane degraded in human CHF and Caroli disease by plasminogen/tPA overexpression. Now add: collagen I excess (fibrotic component) may simultaneously promote abnormal cholangiocyte fate specification via ITGα2β1. Two arms of the ECM story — loss of BM collagen IV AND gain of interstitial collagen I — both pathogenic by different mechanisms.

⚠️ Limits: hiPSC-derived model only. Not tested in Pkhd1-deficient cholangiocytes or ARPKD liver tissue. Developmental interpretation extrapolated from a stem cell differentiation model. Whether excess collagen I in an already-formed fibrotic ARPKD-CHF liver continues to misdirect any remaining progenitors is unknown.

⭐⭐ FINDING — Complete PKHD1 molecular architecture: serpentine model, three genotype-phenotype zones, new domains (C6 cassette, Glu-binding motifs, PbH1 10/11), SEA domain too disordered to evaluate Met3642Ile by stability methods. ⭐⭐ FINDING · Nov 12, 2026

Source: Pillai JP, Puntambekar SN, Onuchic-Whitford AC, Ferreira F, Sampson MG, Onuchic LF, Sayer JA. "Autosomal Recessive Polycystic Kidney Disease: The Complete Molecular Architecture for the PKHD1 Gene and Its Protein." Comput Struct Biotechnol J. 2026;35(1):Article 0261. DOI: 10.34133/csbj.0261. Published November 12, 2026. Boston Children's Hospital / Harvard / Broad / BWH / University of São Paulo / Newcastle. John Sayer is on this newsletter's distribution list.

Structural model: AlphaFold 3 serpentine model of fibrocystin (overall pLDDT 73.59; dimensions 108nm × 36nm × 8nm). Confirms established domains; identifies four new elements: (1) C6 cassette (AA 547-722) — 6 conserved cysteines forming disulfide linkages, galactose-binding domain superfamily (E = 6.19×10⁻²⁶), immediately downstream of PA14; (2) Glu-binding motifs (6 E-X-E sites, AA 1805-1895) — surface-exposed, proposed protein-protein interaction sites at IPT12/G8-1 boundary; (3) PbH1 10 and 11 (AA 3141-3162 and 3163-3184) — two additional PbH1 repeats not in original 2002 description; (4) β-sandwich (AA 3409-3556) between PbH1-11 and SEA. SEA domain pLDDT = 60.65 — lowest confidence of any domain.

Three genotype-phenotype zones (Cox proportional-hazards model, 304 ARegPKD patients, Burgmaier 2021 cohort):

  • Zone 1 (AA 709-1837): Surface-exposed variants → HR 0.19-0.20 for kidney failure (p < 0.005) — kidney protective
  • Zone 2 (AA 1838-2624): Least destabilizing (mean ΔΔG, p = 0.12 vs other classes) → protective hepatic phenotype
  • Zone 3 (AA 2625-4074): G8-2/PbH1 + TM + CTT → HR 4.39 (95% CI 1.21-4.39, p = 0.02) for hepatic complications

⭐ Direct relevance: Both variants in Zone 3. His3124Tyr (AA 3124) falls in the PbH1-like domain between PbH1-9 (ends AA 3108) and newly named PbH1-10 (begins AA 3141) — analogous case study p.A3207T (same region) has ΔΔG = -1.57 kcal/mol, significant destabilization. Met3642Ile (AA 3642) is in SEA domain (pLDDT 60.65) — Pillai explicitly excluded variants on residues with pLDDT < 70, so Met3642Ile was not analyzed by stability methods. This is the third independent confirmation (after Cockburn and Pillai) that the SEA domain loop is structurally too uncertain for stability-based pathogenicity evaluation.

⚠️ Important caveat from the paper: The genotype-phenotype zone correlations do not survive Bonferroni correction for multiple comparisons (Table S1). They are hypothesis-generating, not statistically validated prognostic thresholds. The paper acknowledges this explicitly. ΔΔG predicts kidney failure (p < 0.005) but not portal hypertension (p = 0.08) or hepatic complications (p = 0.27) after adjustment.

New contact: Ana C. Onuchic-Whitford (BCH/Harvard Medical School/Broad Institute/BWH) — daughter of Luiz Onuchic (original PKHD1 discoverer, 2002), trained nephrologist, co-author. Not yet in network. Matthew G. Sampson (BCH/Harvard/Broad) — not yet in network.

Note: This paper (Nov 12) joins Cockburn/Bannell (bioRxiv, Oct 20) and a Frontiers in Molecular Biosciences survey (Nov 3, DOI: 10.3389/fmolb.2026.1877678) — three independent fibrocystin structural papers in three weeks, all using AlphaFold 3. The two primary papers (Cockburn and Pillai) do not cite each other. Sayer confirmed he will add the Cockburn citation in Pillai paper proofs.

⭐ FINDING — TNFRSF12A/TWEAK identified as druggable biliary fibrosis target: FN14 antagonism suppresses cholangiocyte-driven periportal fibrosis in biliary atresia model. FINDING · 2024

Source: Xiao MH et al. "Integrative single-cell and spatial transcriptomic analyses identify a pathogenic cholangiocyte niche and TNFRSF12A as therapeutic target for biliary atresia." Hepatology. DOI: 10.1097/HEP.0000000000001064. PMID: 39178365.

What they found: Integrated scRNA-seq (12 liver tissues) + spatial transcriptomics (4 sections) in biliary atresia. Identified a cholangiocyte-enriched spatial niche with activated HSCs, portal fibroblasts, TREM2+ macrophages, and macrovascular endothelial cells. TNFSF12-TNFRSF12A (TWEAK-FN14) was the dominant signal driving the fibroinflammatory cholangiocyte phenotype. Genetic ablation or FN14 blockade suppressed liver injury, inflammation, and bile duct disease in mouse model. Human biliary organoids from BA patients expressed higher CCL2 in response to TWEAK stimulation, promoting monocyte chemotaxis via CCL2-CCR2.

ARPKD-CHF relevance: TWEAK → FN14 → non-canonical NF-κB → HSC activation + portal fibroblast activation + ductular reaction = periportal fibrosis. This is the inflammatory amplification node of ARPKD-CHF already in the framework (NF-κB node). The cholangiocyte niche described — surrounded by activated HSCs, portal fibroblasts, and TREM2+ macrophages — is an almost exact description of the ARPKD-CHF portal triad. FN14 antagonism with L524-0366 reduced BA model fibrosis. No ARPKD-CHF testing exists.

Framework note: Adds a specific, druggable upstream ligand-receptor pair (TWEAK/FN14) to the existing NF-κB node. Existing antagonist (L524-0366) is a research compound; clinical-stage FN14 antibodies exist in oncology pipeline.

FINDING — CTGF explicitly named as the YAP/TAZ effector in fibrocystin deficiency; senolytic and senomorphic approaches to cholangiocyte SASP in cholangiopathies reviewed. FINDING · Nov 5, 2026

Source: Frontiers in Pediatrics. DOI: 10.3389/fped.2026.1928268. Published November 5, 2026.

Key statement: "In cholangiocytes, fibrocystin deficiency leads to dysregulation of the Hippo signalling pathway, promoting YAP/TAZ nuclear translocation and upregulating CTGF expression, thereby directly driving periportal fibrogenesis." This is the first explicit published statement naming CTGF (CCN2, Connective Tissue Growth Factor) as the specific downstream effector of YAP/TAZ in fibrocystin-deficient cholangiocytes.

ARPKD-CHF relevance: CTGF is a well-validated pro-fibrotic mediator and the target of pamrevlumab (FG-3019, Roche) — a clinical-stage anti-CTGF monoclonal antibody in Phase 3 for IPF and pancreatic cancer. The YAP/TAZ node is established in the ARPKD-CHF framework; CTGF as the specific effector adds a clinically tractable downstream target. The framework also contains a 2019 iPSC-CHF paper (Journal of Hepatology) that independently confirmed: loss of fibrocystin → IL-8 → CTGF → cholangiocyte proliferation + fibrosis in human PKHD1-KO cells, with elevated IL-8 and CTGF confirmed in CHF patient liver samples.

SASP note: The paper also reviews cholangiocyte senescence and SASP (senescence-associated secretory phenotype) in cholangiopathies, identifying IFIT3 (interferon-induced protein with tetratricopeptide repeats 3) as a key SASP regulator in PBC senescent bile duct cells — IFIT3 expression correlated with cholangitis activity and inadequate UDCA response. Whether IFIT3 is elevated in ARPKD-CHF senescent cholangiocytes is an open gap question.

FINDING — P2X7 is a scaffold for NLRP3 (not merely an ion channel): costunolide binds P2X7 extracellular domain (Kd = 3.08 μM, human) and blocks assembly; clinical-stage P2X7 antagonists JNJ-54175446 and JNJ-55308942 in Phase 2. NODE UPGRADE · Nov 2026

Source: Zhang F, Wang Q, Li T et al. "Costunolide-Mediated Modulation of the P2X7–NLRP3 Complex Suppresses Oligodendrocyte Precursor Cell Pyroptosis and Rescues Perinatal White Matter Injury." CNS Neurosci Ther. 2026;32:e71263. DOI: 10.1002/cns.71263. Qingdao University.

Mechanistic upgrade to existing framework node: P2X7-NLRP3 is confirmed in the ARPKD kidney (Arkhipov 2019: P2rx7 KO attenuates cyst growth in PCK rat). This neuroscience paper establishes that P2X7 physically scaffolds NLRP3 at the plasma membrane — a "receptor-anchored inflammasome assembly" model confirmed by reciprocal Co-IP. The ionic signaling (K⁺ efflux, Ca²⁺ influx) is secondary to the protein-protein interaction. Costunolide (sesquiterpene lactone): Kd = 3.08 μM to human P2X7 extracellular ATP-binding domain (SPR confirmed). Suppresses the full Caspase-1/GSDMD/IL-1β axis at 5 mg/kg in vivo. P2rx7 KO rats used here are the same CRISPR/Cas9 model from the ARPKD kidney literature (cited as reference [30] in this paper).

Clinical translation: CNS-penetrant P2X7 antagonist JNJ-54175446 is in Phase 2 for major depressive disorder; JNJ-55308942 is also in clinical development. If P2X7-NLRP3 is confirmed in ARPKD biliary cells, clinical-stage antagonists already exist.

Framework note: P2X7 in ARPKD-CHF biliary cells remains unconfirmed — the question has been routed to Pritchard/Apte (KUMC) who have PCK rat liver RNA-seq (PRJNA799864). Whether P2rx7 appears in their DEG dataset would immediately upgrade this from hypothesis to ARPKD-confirmed.

🧪 GENE THERAPY & BASE EDITING

FINDING — AAV-mediated Sox4 silencing rescues bile duct paucity in Alagille syndrome after disease onset; hepatobiliary intermediate cell cluster (Sox4+Sox9+) identified as the therapeutic target. FINDING · Oct 2025

Source: Fox D, Jafar-Nejad H et al. Gastroenterology 169(5):1000-1016, October 2025. DOI: 10.1053/j.gastro.2025.04.033. PMID: 40446945. Baylor College of Medicine / UMass Chan / Cincinnati Children's (co-investigator Huppert SS).

What they found: AAV8-mediated Sox4 silencing, delivered at postnatal day 15 (after liver necrosis had already appeared), led to functional bile duct formation, reduced scarring, and improved liver function into adulthood in Jag1-heterozygous Alagille mice. scRNA-seq revealed an intermediate hepatobiliary cluster co-expressing Sox4 and Sox9 in diseased livers — cells arrested between hepatocyte and cholangiocyte fates. Sox4 silencing unlocked differentiation of this cluster toward functional cholangiocytes.

ARPKD-CHF relevance — the inverse insight: Alagille has duct paucity (too few); ARPKD-CHF has ductal plate malformation (arrested ductal remodeling, too many remnants). These are opposite structural phenotypes, but the shared insight is the Sox4+/Sox9+ hepatobiliary intermediate cell cluster — cells arrested in a transitional state. In ARPKD-CHF, the DPM may represent Sox4/Sox9 co-expressing cells that failed to complete the Sox4-off, Sox9-on transition required for mature bile duct morphogenesis. No one has looked at Sox4 expression in PKHD1-deficient tissue. Co-investigator Huppert SS is at Cincinnati Children's — your daughter's hospital.

🏥 CLINICAL, PRENATAL & BIOMARKERS

⭐ FINDING — RAFT trial: serial amnioinfusions achieve 91% live birth rate in anhydramnios from fetal kidney failure; 7 of 11 long-term survivors received kidney transplants. ⭐ FINDING · Jul 2026

Source: Miller JL, Baschat AA, Johnson A, Blumenfeld YJ et al. (32 centers). "Neonatal Survival After Serial Amnioinfusions for Anhydramnios Due to Fetal Kidney Failure: The RAFT Clinical Trial." JAMA 336(6):473-483, August 11, 2026. DOI: 10.1001/jama.2026.8568. PMID: 42384373. Free PMC Article. NCT03101891. Christian Hanna (Mayo Clinic) is a co-author and is on this newsletter's distribution list.

What they did: 32 pregnancies with anhydramnios before 22 weeks due to fetal kidney failure (excluding bilateral renal agenesis) enrolled across 13 US fetal therapy centers, December 2018–February 2025. Serial intraperitoneal amnioinfusions initiated before 26 weeks to restore amniotic fluid volume and allow lung development.

Results: 29/32 (91%) live births. All delivered preterm (median 34 weeks 1 day). 65.5% survived to 14 days with surgical dialysis access placement (primary endpoint). Of 11 long-term survivors, 7 received kidney transplants. RAFT 2 is in development.

ARPKD relevance: ARPKD (bilateral null PKHD1 alleles) causes anhydramnios through fetal kidney failure — these infants are precisely the population this trial addresses. The amnioinfusions address pulmonary hypoplasia (the cause of neonatal death); the kidney disease remains and requires transplant. For null/null ARPKD fetuses previously considered incompatible with survival, this intervention may bridge them to neonatal renal support and eventual transplant. The survival boundary at the most severe end of the ARPKD spectrum is moving.

Open question for Hanna: Were ARPKD cases analyzed as a separate diagnostic subgroup within the kidney failure cohort?

⚠️ SAFETY FLAG — Empagliflozin Phase 2 in ADPKD: unexpected kidney volume INCREASE reported. Combined with PCK rat dapagliflozin data: SGLT2 inhibitors may worsen PKD kidney disease regardless of PKD type. ⚠️ SAFETY FLAG · Oct 2026

ADPKD Phase 2 source: Seliger SL, Gitomer B, You Z et al. "Empagliflozin therapy in autosomal dominant polycystic kidney disease: a feasibility randomized controlled trial." J Am Soc Nephrol. Published online October 24, 2026. DOI: 10.1681/ASN.0000001271. PI: Michel Chonchol (CU Anschutz PKD Center). Presented at ASN Kidney Week 2026, Denver. Result: safe and well tolerated, but unexpected increase in kidney volume.

ARPKD PCK rat source: Arkhipov SN, Potter DL, Geurts AM, Pavlov TS. "Knockout of P2rx7 Purinergic Receptor Attenuates Cyst Growth in a Rat Model of ARPKD." Am J Physiol Renal Physiol 317:F1649-F1655, 2019. (This paper was cited by a 2026 Cureus review noting the dapagliflozin result.) Dapagliflozin in PCK rats: 23% increased kidney weight, fourfold albuminuria increase, hyperfiltration, increased cyst volume. Original source: DOI: 10.1371/journal.pone.0125603.

Mechanistic explanation: SGLT2 is expressed in the proximal tubule. SGLT2 inhibition → increased glucose and osmotic load in tubular fluid delivered downstream to distal nephron and collecting duct — the compartment where ARPKD cysts originate. Increased tubular flow to already-cystic segments → cyst expansion. Additionally, SGLT2 inhibitors reduce GFR via tubuloglomerular feedback, but in PKD kidneys with compensatory single-nephron hyperfiltration, this may paradoxically worsen hyperfiltration injury.

Framework implication: SGLT2 inhibitors — including drugs being pursued for ADPKD — may worsen ARPKD kidney disease and should not be assumed safe or beneficial in ARPKD patients or clinical trials. This flag parallels the existing mTOR inhibitor concern (may help kidney while worsening biliary cilia in ARPKD-CHF). The empagliflozin ADPKD kidney volume increase now provides human-trial corroboration for the PCK rat finding.

Network: Michel Chonchol (CU Anschutz PKD Center, PI of empagliflozin trial) is a new contact — not yet in network. Matteus Krappitz is at the same institution.

🫧 ORGANOIDS & DELIVERY PLATFORMS

⭐ FINDING — Human periportal liver assembloid (28 patients): hepatocytes + cholangiocytes + portal mesenchyme in correct spatial architecture models biliary fibrosis. No group has applied this platform to ARPKD patient cells (confirmed by Prof. Huch, September 2026). ⭐ FINDING · 2026

Source: Yuan L, Dawka K, Kim J et al. (Huch M). "Human assembloids recapitulate periportal liver tissue in vitro." Nature 650:438-449, 2026. DOI: 10.1038/s41586-025-09884-1. MPI-CBG Dresden. Companion paper: Dowbaj AM et al. Mouse periportal assembloid. Nature 644:473-482, May 2025. DOI: 10.1038/s41586-025-09183-9. Meritxell Huch (huch@mpi-cbg.de) is on this newsletter's distribution list and has given permission to include both papers.

What they built: Three-cell assembloid combining adult hepatocyte organoids (from 28 different patients), Huch-lab cholangiocyte organoids, and portal liver mesenchyme. Retained periportal histological arrangement, gene expression, and cell-cell interactions through passage 7. Cholangiocytes connect functionally to hepatocyte bile canaliculi. Biliary fibrosis develops when mesenchyme is in excess (20× normal). Patient-specific: fibrosis-associated gene signatures (Acta2, Timp1) are retained through early passages.

ARPKD-CHF relevance: The periportal region — hepatocytes, cholangiocytes, and portal mesenchyme in a spatially organized architecture where bile canaliculi drain into bile ducts and portal fibroblasts govern fibrotic response — is exactly the diseased compartment in ARPKD-CHF. This is the first human model capable of reconstituting this architecture from patient-derived cells. Incorporating PKHD1-mutant cholangiocytes would generate the first comprehensive human model of ARPKD-CHF periportal disease. Prof. Huch confirmed in September 2026 that no group has yet applied this platform to ARPKD patient cells.

GTX-11 (bonus entry): A parallel finding of interest for portal hypertension: GTX-11, a novel compound, improved portal hypertension AND liver fibrosis in MASH by activating p-eNOS (+61%) and reprogramming HSCs toward deactivation. Validated in human liver tissue. Source: JHEP Reports 7(10):101497, July 2025. DOI: 10.1016/j.jhepr.2025.101497. No ARPKD or CHF data yet, but eNOS pathway is relevant to intrahepatic portal resistance in ARPKD-CHF.

Open Questions

Open Questions for the Research Community

These questions came out of this month's reading and are not answered in the sources we found. If your work addresses any of them — preliminary data, an unpublished observation, or a paper we missed — a brief reply is all we ask.

GENETICS & VARIANTS
Q1. Which PKHD1 variants cause liver-predominant disease? Cohorts so far (Burgmaier's zones, Zhao, Lapunzina-Soler, and Bhuva's nine congenital hepatic fibrosis cases) have not been pooled into one table of variant, position and phenotype. Do missense variants in the C-terminal ectodomain (Zone 3, AA 2625–4074) cluster in liver-predominant cases? A 2025 series of prenatally diagnosed Caroli-type cases found three of four missense variants in this region, but the numbers are tiny. An older French cohort (Denamur 2010) lists missense variants in the same region (W2736G, I2957T, R3240K) among children who died perinatally, so the region is not liver-only. Small case reports point both ways (Giacobbe 2022; and a prenatally diagnosed Caroli case with one missense variant in this region who needed kidney and liver transplants by 16 months, Rivas 2019).
Q2. Does an interior-exon missense variant of uncertain significance, such as c.10926G>A (p.Met3642Ile), change splicing? Precedents exist (R760H through an enhancer motif; a last-nucleotide missense with exon 40 skipping). Would a minigene assay or urine-cell RNA testing settle it? (A SpliceAI and Pangolin lookup on Oct 8 predicted no splice effect, with all scores 0.05 or lower; these tools miss enhancer-mediated skipping.)
Q3. Do modifier genes beyond PKHD1 explain the wide variation in ARPKD severity, including liver involvement? Does the UK PKD genetics fellowship's dataset include ARPKD patients? Candidates raised in the older literature, from animal work, include HNF1B, KIF12 and interactions with PKD1 and PKD2; human evidence is lacking. A 2018 mouse study found that a truncating Pkhd1 variant caused clinical biliary disease only on the NOD autoimmune background (Huang et al.).
DELIVERY & THERAPY
Q4. How can cholangiocytes be reached? Five clinical AAV capsids transduced them poorly in perfused human livers. Has anyone screened capsids or tried retrograde bile-duct delivery for cholangiocytes? A 2026 antibody report (pIgR-engaging bispecifics, abstract level) names ARPKD in its introduction as a disease hard to reach with circulating antibodies.
Q5. Is there a primary, citable report of AAV-associated liver failure in children with pre-existing cholangiopathy, and does it apply to congenital hepatic fibrosis? The 2020 editorial on the myotubular myopathy deaths describes liver toxicity at high dose but does not mention pre-existing cholangiopathy.
Q6. Would activating long-form PDE4 (LoAc® compounds) reduce cyst growth in ARPKD cholangiocytes without worsening stellate-cell fibrosis? Published PDE4D data point the other way for fibrosis.
BIOMARKERS & TRIALS
Q7. What liver endpoint could regulators accept for ARPKD-CHF: a stiffness trend, platelet count and spleen size, a blood marker, or an imaging tracer? What are the pediatric reference ranges, and how should values from different elastography methods be compared?
Q8. Is PKHD1 detectable in healthy children's cholangiocytes in the pediatric liver atlas, and do adult-derived findings (for example the OLR1+ macrophages) hold at a pediatric baseline?
MODELS & CELLS
Q9. Can urine-derived cells from children with ARPKD-CHF yield cholangiocyte or assembloid models, and which labs are set up to receive samples with proper consent? A 2018 study used urine-derived kidney epithelial cells to test splicing of ciliopathy-gene variants (Molinari et al., cited in a 2026 review).
IMMUNE–CHOLANGIOCYTE INTERACTIONS
Q10. Do PKHD1-deficient human cholangiocytes and ARPKD-CHF livers show immune interactions like the T cell–cholangiocyte CD226–NECTIN2 signal found in the Pkhd1-dependent NOD.c3c4 mouse? Is peribiliary T-cell infiltration part of ARPKD-CHF, and could immune-modulating antibodies be tested in that model?
CELL THERAPY
Q11. Could patient-derived, gene-corrected cholangiocyte organoids be delivered into the bile ducts of a Pkhd1-deficient model, and would the corrected cells engraft and persist in cystic, fibrotic ducts as they do in healthy or acutely injured ones?

NOTE — From the PKD Foundation Patient Summit (September 2026): Kaplan (Yale) on NIH funding and immigrant scientists. NOTE · Sep 2026

At the PKD Foundation Patient Summit in September 2026, Prof. Michael Kaplan (Yale, Lilian Jean Kaplan Prize 2024, co-founder Estuary Biotherapeutics) made two statements that this newsletter believes deserve wider distribution: "As academic scientists, we are terrified of what is happening at the NIH. And to whatever extent you can speak in favor of support for medical research that is reviewed and funded based solely on excellence and not politics, I strongly urge you to do that." And: "None of them [the postdocs who did this work] were born in the United States. So an openness to the people from outside this country who are brilliant scientists and who want to do this work is incredibly important." Patient families are among the most credible voices for NIH funding advocacy. We pass this along without further comment.

Scientific note: Kaplan's PC1 C-terminal tail (200aa) × NNT/mitochondria work is AAV-deliverable and suppresses PKD2 disease in a PC1-KO model (unpublished). Clinical trials for PKD2 gene therapy and compact PC1 estimated within ~3 years. ⭐ FINDING — CRISPRa of endogenous PKD1 achieves 2–2.5-fold upregulation and rescues proliferation, cyst formation, mitochondrial function, and cAMP in ADPKD cells: direct proof-of-concept for Sayer's PKHD1 enhancer manipulation proposal. ⭐ FINDING · Nov 13, 2026

Source: Chakraborty A, Varghese MM, Wallace DP, Ward CJ, Yu ASL (corresponding). University of Kansas Medical Center. Preprint DOI: 10.64898/2026.05.23.727418, May 23, 2026.

What they did: Screened 11 human PKD1-sgRNAs and 15 mouse Pkd1-sgRNAs targeting the PKD1 proximal promoter (500 bp window) using a dCas9-VPR platform. Best guides: sgRNA12 and sgRNA15 (2.75-to-4-fold induction in human cells; up to 13.66-fold in mouse). In Pkd1RC/− collecting duct cells: 2–2.5-fold upregulation of Pkd1 mRNA; PC1 protein confirmed at 460 kDa. Effects: significantly reduced proliferation (p<0.05), reduced 3D microcyst formation (p=0.039), improved mitochondrial membrane potential, reduced aerobic glycolysis (Warburg effect), reduced intracellular cAMP (p=0.03), reduced cMyc/pCreb/pErk, and increased pYap1 (inactive/cytoplasmic YAP). Validated in primary mouse PKD cells and human NHK cells. Human ADPKD donors: 2/5 responded robustly.

ARPKD-CHF implications: (1) This is the published proof-of-concept for the approach John Sayer described to us in October — "manipulation of enhancers etc." for PKHD1 therapy. The analogous experiment (CRISPRa of PKHD1 promoter in ARPKD biliary cells) has not been done. (2) The pYap1 increase confirms that polycystin restoration actively suppresses YAP nuclear activity — supporting the YAP-dependent pathway for CTGF in polycystin-deficient cells. (3) 2–2.5-fold upregulation = sufficient rescue in ADPKD; if biliary ARPKD disease requires less FPC restoration than kidney disease (supported by Pillai 2026 — ΔΔG predicts kidney, not hepatic, severity), then PKHD1 CRISPRa needs even less than the 2-fold mark to prevent periportal fibrosis. (4) Safety caveat: PKD1 overexpression at 2-to-15-fold above wild-type causes cystic disease in mice — single-guide with moderate activation only. (5) New KUMC contact: Prof. Alan S.L. Yu (consulting: Estuary, Calico, Novartis/Regulus, Torque Bio). Same institution as Pritchard/Apte (PCK rat RNA-seq paper) and David Wallace (YAP in ARPKD-CHF hepatic cysts, Jiang et al. 2017).

FINDING — Metformin promotes cholangiocyte regeneration in cholestatic fibrosis via HK1-glycolysis-dependent epigenetic activation of LGR5: a new drug repurposing signal for biliary disease. FINDING · Nov 14, 2026

Liang Y, Lu Q, Ye Y et al.; Tam PKH (corresponding). MedComm 2026;7:e71143. DOI: 10.1002/mco2.71143. Published November 12, 2026. BDL and Mdr2−/− mouse models + BDL-derived cholangiocyte organoids + patient-derived BA organoids. Fotios Sampaziotis (Wellcome-MRC Cambridge) is a co-author. Key mechanism: metformin → HK1 upregulation → glycolysis → elevated acetyl-CoA → H4K16 acetylation at LGR5 promoter TSS → LGR5 transcription → cholangiocyte regeneration. This is AMPK-independent. Metformin also increases Cdk1/Cdk2 in regenerating cholangiocytes — confirming the double-edged nature of CDK inhibition in biliary disease.

FINDING — CXCL9 reverse zonation in hepatic congestion: AMG-487 (CXCR3 inhibitor) reduces portal pressures and fibrosis in congestive hepatopathy model. FINDING · Nov 16, 2026

FINDING — ARPKD pulmonary hypertension → right heart failure → centrolobular fibrosis: a second fibrosis mechanism additive to periportal ARPKD-CHF fibrosis; echocardiographic screening recommended. FINDING · Nov 14, 2026

Hamberger F, Hansmann G et al. Front Cardiovasc Med 9:862330, 2022. DOI: 10.3389/fcvm.2022.862330. Hannover Medical School. PAH → right heart failure → elevated central venous pressure → peri-central hepatic congestion → HSC activation → zone 3 centrilobular fibrosis confirmed in SuHx rat model and human PAH tissue. For ARPKD-CHF: pulmonary hypertension is a known ARPKD complication. When present, two anatomically distinct fibrosis patterns operate simultaneously — zone 1 periportal (from cholangiocyte SPP1/HSC activation) and zone 3 centrilobular (from venous congestion). FINDING — IL17A is 218 kb from PKHD1 on chromosome 6p12.2 and likely shares chromatin architecture: a CRISPRa safety check for all PKHD1 enhancer manipulation protocols. FINDING · Nov 14, 2026

GRCh38 coordinates: PKHD1 ends at chr6:51,968,629; IL17A begins at chr6:52,186,375 — gap of approximately 218 kb. At this separation, within the typical TAD range of 200-400 kb, these two genes almost certainly share chromatin architecture. IL17A is the master Th17 cytokine driving periductal inflammation in PSC, PBC, and biliary atresia. Any PKHD1 CRISPRa or enhancer manipulation protocol that upregulates PKHD1 transcription could bystander-activate IL17A if the shared TAD includes IL17A regulatory elements. The safety experiment required before PKHD1 CRISPRa proceeds to cells is an IL17A expression assay — equivalent to the Rab26 bystander check performed by Yu/KUMC for PKD1 CRISPRa. This does not preclude Track C therapy; it defines the safety protocol required. Routing: Sayer (Newcastle, enhancer manipulation) and Yu ASL (KUMC, CRISPRa of PKD1).

FINDING — CDK1 inhibition has a double-edged risk in biliary disease: Wnt7b-driven regenerative cholangiocyte proliferation uses the same CDK axis as pathological biliary proliferation. FINDING · Nov 16, 2026

Ma X, Yin Y, Fan Y et al. Front Bioeng Biotechnol 14:1918151, 2026. DOI: 10.3389/fbioe.2026.1918151. Shengjing Hospital, China Medical University. Cholangiocytes upregulate Wnt7b following bile duct injury, driving regenerative proliferation. Inhibiting Wnt secretion reduced mitosis at 24h but failed to alter collagen accumulation at 48h — broad Wnt/CDK inhibition suppresses beneficial epithelial repair while failing to prevent fibrosis. Independently confirmed: Liang et al. MedComm 2026 showed metformin increases Cdk1 and Cdk2 in regenerating cholangiocyte organoids. CDK1 is overactivated in ARPKD-CHF (pathological) but also upregulated during Wnt-mediated biliary repair (regenerative). The therapeutic window for CDK1 inhibition requires distinguishing pathological from regenerative proliferation. Senolytics (dasatinib + quercetin) in bile duct machine perfusion: 8/9 recipients were stricture-free at 222 days — first clinical application of senolytics for biliary disease.

⚠️ SAFETY FLAG UPDATE — SGLT2 inhibitors: three independent harm mechanisms in ARPKD kidney; KDIGO 2025 does not recommend in ADPKD including for diabetes; human Phase 2 empagliflozin trial showed kidney volume INCREASE. SAFETY UPDATE · Nov 14, 2026

Three harm mechanisms now documented — all converging on the collecting duct (cyst-origin segment in ARPKD): (1) Vasopressin/cAMP: SGLT2 inhibition → glycosuria → osmotic diuresis → compensatory vasopressin → V2 receptor in collecting duct → ↑ cAMP → cyst growth. (2) Downstream solute delivery: blocked proximal reabsorption → more solute delivered to already-diseased collecting duct. (3) Direct collecting duct hypertrophy: SGLT2i enlarges kidneys ~13% via S3 proximal tubule + collecting duct hypertrophy (Sinha Front Pharmacol 2023). KDIGO 2025 guideline: does not currently recommend SGLT2i in ADPKD including for diabetes, because safety not evaluated. Kanbay 2026 meta-analysis (6 studies, 451 ADPKD patients): insufficient evidence for kidney benefit. Human signal: Seliger/Chonchol JASN Oct 24, 2026 — empagliflozin in ADPKD showed unexpected kidney volume INCREASE. PCK rat (ARPKD model) dapagliflozin: +23% kidney weight, +100% cyst volume, 4× albuminuria.

FINDING — O-mannosylation of fibrocystin by TMEM260 at IPT domains: a fourth pathogenic mechanism class for PKHD1 missense variants; L1407R abolishes O-mannosylation and explains SHDRA renal phenotype. FINDING · Sep 22, 2026

Kaynert J, Hopf C, Brand-Merseburger S, Garbers C, Pich A, Buettner FFR (corresponding). bioRxiv DOI: 10.64898/2026.09.21.753134. September 22, 2026. Hannover Medical School / University of Augsburg. Fibrocystin is O-mannosylated by TMEM260 at IPT9 (AA ~1407, confirmed), IPT7, and IPT5 (weakly). L1407R (ARPKD patient variant in IPT9) completely abolishes O-mannosylation of the adjacent threonine — the leucine at position −1 of the TMEM260 recognition motif is required for substrate recognition. TMEM260 loss-of-function causes SHDRA syndrome, whose renal anomalies phenocopy ARPKD — now explained by impaired FPC O-mannosylation. This establishes a fourth pathogenic mechanism class: (1) surface PPI disruption (Zone 1), (2) misfolding/destabilization (Zone 2-3), (3) SEA cleavage disruption, (4) O-mannosylation site abolishment (Zone 2 IPT at TMEM260 sequon −1). Our daughter's variants (His3124Tyr Zone 3, Met3642Ile SEA domain) are outside the confirmed IPT O-mannosylation sites.

TOOL NOTE — MissenseHMM: a Hidden Markov Model combining 43 missense pathogenicity scores to annotate 70+ million variants — useful for most PKHD1 missense variants but cannot evaluate intrinsically disordered domains like the SEA domain. TOOL · Nov 19, 2026

Li R, Ernst J. DOI: 10.64898/2026.01.31.703062. February 2026. The tool assigns each of 70+ million human missense variants to one of 20 HMM states based on combinatorial patterns from 43 pathogenicity predictors in dbNSFP, capturing spatial context via transition parameters. State annotations enhanced individual predictors' associations with ClinVar variants and deep mutational scanning data beyond any single tool. All variants pre-annotated — lookup without rerunning predictors. Critical caveat for ARPKD-CHF: All 43 constituent predictors use protein stability frameworks. For variants in intrinsically disordered regions — such as the PKHD1 SEA domain (pLDDT 60.65, excluded from Pillai's ΔΔG analysis) — the ensemble correctly synthesizes predictor consensus but that consensus is incorrect by mechanism, not by statistics. MissenseHMM is reliable for stability-domain variants (his3124Tyr class) and unreliable for functional-domain variants (Met3642Ile SEA loop class) for the same underlying reason every individual predictor fails there.
